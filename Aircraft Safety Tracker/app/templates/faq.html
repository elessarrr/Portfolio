International Investigations

Some incidents in this database include an NTSB record ID but do not have a published NTSB docket. This is most common for international events where the NTSB is a participating or accredited representative, not the lead investigating authority.

Under ICAO Annex 13, the State of Occurrence is responsible for leading the investigation and deciding what public materials are released. In those cases, the NTSB may publish limited metadata but may not publish a U.S. docket package.

When our system detects this condition, the page may show: "No official NTSB docket - why?" linked to this section.

If the NTSB docket endpoint returns "The docket for this investigation has not been released.", we treat that as a stable non-publish state and suppress the broken/unhelpful link in incident views.
